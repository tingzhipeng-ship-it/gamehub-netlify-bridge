⚪ GameHub Mini

SH1106 128 × 64GAMEHUB MINIGames • Settings • Wi-Fi

Confirmed OLED wiring

SDA → GPIO21, SCL → GPIO22, VCC → 3V3, GND → GND. Buttons: UP 32, DOWN 33, LEFT 25, RIGHT 26, A 27, B 14, START 18, SELECT 19. All buttons active-low to GND.

Buzzer GPIO13 and battery ADC GPIO34 are proposed, not hardware-verified. Battery voltage must be safely divided before GPIO34.
