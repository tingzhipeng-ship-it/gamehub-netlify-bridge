WIRELESS PAIRING

Pair your Game Boy by PIN

Choose your 1.3-inch OLED or 2.8-inch prototype, then enter the six-digit PIN shown on your ESP32. Your Game Boy must have an internet connection and the Netlify bridge must be deployed for pairing to work.

Open PIN pairing →Game downloads ↗

Your ESP32 can still play Snake and Pong offline. No account is required for the browser games.
